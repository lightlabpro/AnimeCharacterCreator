# 21 Draw (Learn from 100 famous artists) - reading notes (q20)

Read: full text layer (every page, 251 pp) + every page image (28 3x3 sheets) + full-size head rows of p14, p158,
p188. The book is a character-design reference: per character a page of 3 head views + front/side turnaround and a
page of action poses and hands, plus 13 step-by-step tutorials. Very little text.

## Text content (all of it)
- Intro p8-9: built on artist surveys - people want figure drawing, character types, action poses, faces, hands;
  learn by imitating high-quality references plus some process text.
- Tutorials (p72-75 Tunjic fairy; p92-95 Porter goddess; p102-105 Ilic Hercules; p108-111 Bardin hero front/side;
  p112-115 Steve Rude; p134-137 Schmidt Medusa; p152-155 Reiq ninja; p160-163 Bowater peasant; p190-193 Greene;
  p206-209 Bills survivalist; p222-225 Delgado viking girl; p232-235 Phobs; p240-247 Sandoval zombie). Points that
  bear on our head:
  - Flip the image horizontally to catch features that "melt" to one side (Tunjic p72) - applies to our 3/4 mouth
    drift: check mirrored renders.
  - Steve Rude p114: "know how the overall skull looks from every angle, then fit the features piece by piece into
    that skull shape. That's the secret." Features are placed ON the skull form, not pasted on a flat mask.
  - Reiq p153: shade with 3 grey values only (light, mid, dark) - too many values break the balance.
  - Ilic p103 / Bowater p163 / Delgado p225: flat shadows as big simple shapes from one light source; shaded areas
    are areas hidden from the light, not simply dark-coloured areas.
  - Bardin p109: thicker lines on the underside of forms give weight and dimension.

## Images (all 100 characters viewed)
- Anime/manga-style turnarounds: Adventurer (Moriarty p14), Elf (Genzoman p68), Fighter (Chamba p82), Goth girl
  (Ein Lee p96), Kawaii Tokyo girl (p120), Kung-fu girl (Qinni p124), Outlaw (Gokunobaka p158), School diva
  (Meago p188), Ninja (Reiq p152-155), Sorceress (Shilin p196). Others are western cartoon, painterly or creature
  designs.
- What the anime 3/4 heads show (p14, p124, p158, p188 at full size):
  - The open mouth sits almost entirely on the near side of the face centre, close under the near cheek; the far
    half of the mouth is a short stub that tucks behind the cheek silhouette.
  - The far eye is narrower (compressed, about 2/3 of the near eye) and its outer end touches or is cut by the
    face contour; the near eye keeps its full almond.
  - The far cheek bulges out past the far eye line, then the jaw runs in a straight diagonal to a small chin.
  - Mouth line is about midway nose-to-chin; chin is short and pointed; the lower face does not hang.
  - Open mouths are simple D or trapezoid shapes, dark interior, one upper teeth band at most, a tongue shape at the
    bottom; the upper lip is not drawn as a line - only the top edge of the opening plus a short shadow.
  - Nose is a small tick/shadow on the near side; in profile a small point.
- Side views (front/side turnarounds on most character pages): the profile eye is a short wedge/triangle open to the
  front, set back from the nose bridge; never a frontal almond.

## What this means for head_mhs3_n56 (q20)
- 3/4 mouth must shift toward the near cheek and the far half must foreshorten hard (matches Edwards p229, Crilley
  p36/80, animeoutline).
- Far eye compress/narrow and wedge-like in profile (fixes the "Egyptian" look).
- Keep toon shading at 2-3 values with one light; give the upper lip / under-nose plane a shadow shape.
- Validate mirrored renders for side drift.
