# Mastering Manga with Mark Crilley (414 pp) - reading notes (q19)

Read: full text (OCR, 369 image-only pages) + every page image (2x2 sheets). Notes paraphrase; page numbers are PDF pages.

## Head construction (front) - p19-32, p41-49
- Every head starts from a circle with a cross; the lower half of the circle is split into four equal bands by three
  lines: brows on line 1, upper lash line on line 2, irises reach line 3 (p26, p43).
- Chin below the circle by 1/4 of the diameter (teen, p27, p44); adults 1/3 (p69), with brows and eyes one band higher
  (p70). Jaw lines leave the circle sides at about the brow line and run nearly straight to a soft point (p27 figure).
- Upper lash lines touch the circle edge at each side; one eye width of blank space between the eyes (p28, p45).
- Nose rests on the bottom of the circle; mouth midway between the circle bottom and the chin tip (p28, p45).
- Ear: top level with the middle of the eye, bottom just above the mouth line (p29, p46).
- Hair line about 1/4 diameter above the circle - manga heads are top heavy (p30).
- Male vs female: almost the same face; the male's upper lash line is a little thinner (p49).
- Style spectrum (p19-21): realistic (ears at eye level), slightly cartoony (bigger eyes, nose/mouth less detailed,
  eye-to-nose distance near real), very cartoony (ears level with the nose); bigger eyes go with a smaller,
  de-emphasised nose and mouth - "it's not just the eyes get bigger".
- Eyes (p13-17): three slightly-taller-than-wide boxes between two lines; lash lines tilt up toward the centre; upper
  lash curve stronger; iris with a highlight at the top; the iris oval flattens where it meets the lower lash;
  lid-fold line above the inner corner only (p29, p46).
- Shadow under the chin is the one shadow every lesson adds (p31, p39, p48, p58 ...).

## Three-quarter view - p33-40, p75-84, p95-104
- The 3/4 is the most important manga view (p33).
- A gently curving centre line runs from the chin up to the brow line, off to the turned side (p35); eyes, brows, nose
  and mouth all touch that curve (p36); the far brow is not directly over the far eye but a little to one side (p36).
- In the figure the mouth starts at the centre curve and extends toward the NEAR side; the far eye is narrow and sits
  against the far cheek edge; the far contour follows the circle down to the brow line and then the cheek/jaw line
  angles in to the chin (p36-37 figures).
- Adult 3/4 (p79-80): the upper cheek begins where the 2nd line meets the circle; the nose tip falls between the 3rd
  and 4th lines at the circle edge; the mouth a little less than halfway between chin and nose, just outside the
  circle edge; one line under each eye and a line under the mouth define maturity (p81).
- Neck: meets the ear/cheek intersection on one side and the chin tip on the other (p38).

## Profile - p50-59, p115-123
- Face line leaves the circle between lines 2 and 3, nose tip just above the bottom line, then down to the chin with
  small lip bumps midway; jaw line points back toward the bottom of the vertical line (p54).
- The eye sits between lines 2 and 3, close to (not touching) the circle edge; the ear between lines 2 and 4, flat
  against the vertical line; mouth close to midway between nose and chin (p55).
- Profile figure (p55-59): the face line from brow to nose tip is a straight slope; below the nose a short
  concave dip, a small upper-lip bump, a smaller lower-lip bump, then the chin; mouth is a short stroke set back
  from the lip bumps; the eye is a narrow wedge/oval behind the face line.

## Eyes and expressions - p124-141
- 101 eyes: semirealistic (small, narrow, close to anatomy) through big shojo eyes; at least one highlight, top
  and/or bottom of the iris (p126).
- Cheerful (p130): small gentle smile; bottoms of the eyes flattened (cheeks rising).
- Ecstatic joy (p131): squeezed-shut eyes; the bottom of the open mouth may be left unrendered.
- Determination (p136): clenched teeth with a break in the mouth outline; anger (p137): wide open mouth, bared
  teeth (p137 figure: a white upper teeth band with a small fang; dark interior below).
- Surprise (p139): small irises floating in the whites, small open gasping mouth.
- Sadness (p141): big irises tucked under the upper lids; mouth with a quivering lower lip.
- Open mouths in the figures (p131, p133, p137, p138): a rounded D or lens; a single upper teeth band; a flat-filled
  interior tone; in colour the interior is one flat warm grey/brown - no gradient, no tongue detail in small heads.

## Adults - p65-84, p173, p183-185
- Adult manga characters are drawn closer to real anatomy: smaller eyes, more rendered nose, longer chin (1/3 dia),
  eyes one band higher; one line under each eye, cheekbone lines and a line beside the mouth add age (p70-81).
- Keep lines near eyes and mouth subtle for a youthful look (p74 "less is more"; p183).

## Bodies, poses, clothing, perspective, inking, panels (p142-409)
- Teen girl 7 heads, teen boy 7 lines (shoulders ~2 heads wide), father figure 8 heads, kid 6, baby 4, chibi 1.5-4
  (p144-224). Male neck thicker, closer to the jaw (p163).
- Clothing folds (p310-330), perspective (p332-366), inking: manga inks with thin, even lines (p367); crosshatching,
  speed lines, panels, balloons, sound effects (p377-409). Not used for the head assets beyond the line-weight note.

## What this means for our head (interpretation)
- Lower face length: chin 1/4 of the skull circle below it for a teen, up to 1/3 for an adult; mouth midway
  between the circle bottom (nose) and the chin. Check ours: a stretched lower face = chin too far below the nose.
- 3/4: the face centre line curves; mouth lies mostly on the near side of it, far brow is offset outward, far eye
  narrow and against the far contour; the far cheek contour bulges out past the eye line then cuts in to the chin.
- Profile eye is a narrow wedge, not a stretched almond.
- Open mouth: one flat interior tone, a thin upper teeth band; no lower teeth or detail at normal head size.
