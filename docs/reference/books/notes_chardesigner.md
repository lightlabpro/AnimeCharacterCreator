# The Character Designer - 21 Draw, 11 artists (194 pp) - reading notes (q20)

Read: full text layer (every page, 3 chunks) + every page image (22 3x3 sheets), plus a close look at p36 (Chamba
front/side head guides).

## Core content
- Randy Bishop, shape language: square = physical/stable, circle = soft/kind, triangle = movement/danger; a cast
  shares one shape "accent".
- Tom Bancroft, gesture: sketching is sculpting ("don't draw lines, draw shapes with lines"); tilts, flow (convex to
  concave outlines), rhythm, twists; line of action as the spine; S and C curves.
- Chamba, heads: front and side circles; eyes at 1/2 height, hairline 1/6 from top, brow 1/6 below hairline, mouth
  in the lower part (Part 1: nose-to-chin split in thirds, mouth at the first third); eyes one eye-width apart;
  "SIDE VIEW: both the eye and ear should stay within the quarter dividing lines" (p36) - the profile eye is drawn
  as a small "<" wedge one quarter back from the front; jawline to the outer eye/brow line at mouth level; chin
  aligns with the mouth's peak in profile; shifting guides up/down changes the style (lower guides = cuter/younger).
- Rene Cordova, female forms: quick pen sketches, 3D volumes, ~7 heads, centre line, front/side/back.
- Michael Bills, turnarounds: ground plane, centre axis, horizontal guides keep every view aligned.
- Loish, colour: one base colour then add, coloured (not black) line art, colours are relative, warm vs cool.
- Wouter Tulp, squash and stretch: keep volume; stretched side simple, squashed side complex; follow perspective;
  facial expressions are squash/stretch of hard and soft parts.
- Rodgon, ageing: proportions per age; "THE STRETCHING JAW METHOD ... stretching the jaw down will age a character
  and ... moving the jaw up" makes it younger (p120); lines and details add years.
- Gerardo Sandoval, mecha: human anatomy under mechanical parts; thicker lines on the prominent contours.
- Kenneth Anderson, cast: archetypes, unique silhouettes, shape language and colour per role; check greyscale.
- LoopyDave, caricature: find the widest point, weight distribution ("squishy balloon"), asymmetry; lips - follow the
  centre line; "avoid painting in all the teeth, you can simply hint at their shape".

## What this means for head_mhs3_n56 (q20)
- "The lower part of the face feels stretched down": a long lower face reads older (Rodgon). Raising the chin /
  moving the mouth so the mouth-to-chin span shrinks keeps the young-adult anime read.
- Profile eye: a small wedge inside the front quarter of the head, not a full almond (Chamba p36, manga12 p119).
- Teeth: a hinted band, not individual teeth (LoopyDave) - our single shaded band is right in kind.
