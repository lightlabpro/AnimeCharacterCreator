# The Complete Guide to Drawing Action Manga — head/face notes

- Title as printed on cover (p0001): *The Complete Guide to Drawing Action Manga: A Step-by-Step Artist's Handbook* — shoco & Makoto Sawa (Tuttle).
- Page count: 150 scanned pages (p0001–p0150), image-only (no text layer). Printed page number = pNNNN − 3.
- How it was read: all 10 contact sheets (sheets4) skimmed; every page with head/face/expression content opened at full resolution, cropped and upscaled 2–3x to read captions and drawings. Page numbers below are pNNNN indices.
- Scope warning: this is a body/pose book. Head and face teaching is thin: no head-construction grid and no turnaround views. Face content is mostly two "Getting the Facial Expression Right" spotlights (p121, p145), a teeth key point (p95), a hair spotlight (p103–105), and expression captions scattered through the pose pages. Rules file: `rules_actionanime_face.json` (73 rules).

## Head shape / face by type
- p15: Male = angular, linear forms (longer face, jaw corner, tapered chin, thick neck with tendons). Female = rounded, soft curves (rounder lower face, small chin, slim neck). Both use the same feature layout: eyes near mid-head, small nose, short mouth.

## Eyes
- p15: neutral almond eyes about one eye-width apart, with a heavier upper lash line.
- p121: rage = wide-open eyes, small irises with white all around, sharply outlined; anger angles the upper lid down toward the nose.
- p145: hit reactions either squeeze the eyes shut or roll the irises up under the lid; comic version pops the eyes out with tiny pupils.
- p132: whites only (no iris) = losing consciousness.
- p143–144 (colour): iris darker at top, lighter below, small highlight; airbrushed glow over the iris; small irises for intensity.

## Brows
- p121: rage = steep V; crying = inner ends up, outer ends down.
- p92: in a glare, raised brows versus a creased gap between the brows change the read. p126: creases between the brows plus sweat = danger/tension.

## Nose / mouth / teeth / tongue
- p15: minimal nose mark; short mouth drawn as one line.
- p95 key point: the gap between the front teeth sits on the face's center line; the canine is the 3rd tooth from the middle; small gaps between teeth; open mouth shows both teeth rows and the tongue.
- p14 (gallery): pointed canines and an extended tongue for wild characters.
- p121: straight mouth = serious; courageous smile = levity; a wide-open mouth makes rage and screams.

## Ears / jaw / neck
- p15: ears run from brow/eye height to the base of the nose; male neck thicker.
- p31: a slight chin lift reads as rising/triumph. p132: an uppercut throws the head back to show the underside of the jaw. p126: when ducking, the head tucks toward the chest. p131: the head turns away from the impact; the stronger the hit, the more extreme the angle.

## Hair framing
- p103/p105: hair grows from the crown and hairline; bangs fall forward when the head drops; asymmetric bangs over the eyes look mysterious; hair stays bouncy even without wind; long side locks drift forward.
- p104: hair trails the motion and whips across the face in spins.

## Face shading
- p143: pick a light source (front and slightly above). Shade under the bangs, nose and chin; put highlights only on protruding areas. p144: scars as accents. p121/p145: blush or hatching on the nose and cheeks for crying, and on the struck area for hits.

## Expressions
- Happy/laugh: p31 (eyes closed into upward arcs, big open mouth, face tilted up), p98 (bright smiles).
- Angry: p121 (rage x2, battle scream), p28 (squeezed slanted eyes, clenched teeth, anger mark), p92 (glare).
- Sad/crying: p121 (drooping brows, open wavy mouth, huge tears, runny nose, blush); holding back emotion (clenched teeth, quiver, brow crease).
- Scared/alarm: p29 (panic scream; the expression alone flips pursuer and victim), p126 (brow creases, sweat), p104 (alert gaze toward the threat).
- Determined: p121 (comic dot eyes, straight serious mouth, battle-ready face), p27 (chasing shout), p127 (enduring with gritted teeth), p143 (signature grin with slanted brows and clenched teeth).
- Pain/hit/KO: p145, p131, p132.
