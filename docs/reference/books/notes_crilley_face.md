# Mastering Manga with Mark Crilley: head, face and expression notes (deep pass)

How this was read: I used the OCR text (text.json) to find every head/face/expression page, then opened each of those
page images (step pages through the 2x2 contact sheets, finished drawings and all expression pages at full size, and
chibi faces cropped and enlarged). Everything here is paraphrased. Page numbers are PDF pages. Rules are in
`rules_crilley_face.json` (110 rules). This pass goes deeper than `notes_crilley.md` / `rules_crilley.json` (17
general rules) and covers the head only.

Units: "circle" = the skull circle every head starts from; "band" = 1/8 of its diameter (the lower half of the circle
is split into 4 equal bands by 3 lines).

## head_shape
- The head is always built on a circle with a cross through it (p19, p25). The lower half of the circle is split into 4 bands:
  brows on line 1, upper lash line on line 2, bottom of the iris on line 3 (p26, p43).
- Teen chin: 1/4 of the diameter below the circle. Jaw lines leave the circle near the brow line and run almost straight
  to a soft point (p27, p44).
- The upper lash lines touch the circle's sides, so the face at eye level is as wide as the skull (p28).
- Hair top: about 1/4 of the diameter above the circle. Manga heads are top heavy (p30).
- 3/4 view (p33-40): a curved centre line runs from the chin up to the brow line, set toward the far side. Brows, eyes,
  nose and mouth all touch it. The far contour follows the circle to the brow line, then the cheek angles in to the chin.
- Profile (p52-59): the face line leaves the circle between lines 2 and 3, a straight slope runs to a nose tip just above
  the bottom line, then come two small lip bumps and a pointed chin. The jaw points back toward the bottom of the vertical line.
- Style spectrum (p19-21): realistic, then slightly cartoony (eye-to-nose distance still close to real), then very cartoony
  (ears at nose level), then shojo (brows very high). Bigger eyes need a smaller nose and mouth.

## face_by_type
- Teen male vs female (p41, p49): the same face. Only the thinner upper lash line differs, plus hair. Long-haired boys get
  a thicker neck and angular features (p63). Boys' necks are thicker and start closer to the jaw (p163).
- Adult (p65-84): chin 1/3 of the diameter below the circle, squarer cheek/jaw, brows and eyes one band higher, eyes
  smaller. The nose is fully drawn (bridge and nostrils). Crow's-feet, one line under each eye and lines beside the mouth
  give age. Adult 3/4 (p79-81): the upper cheek starts where line 2 meets the circle, the nose tip sits between lines 3 and 4
  at the circle edge, and the mouth is a little under halfway from chin to nose. The finished drawing (p84) shows narrow
  almond eyes under thick, low, straight brows. More adult types: p183 (mother, subtle lines), p184 (elderly wrinkles),
  p185 (villain: prominent nose, sunken cheeks, scowl).
- Fuller figure (p85-104): a rounded U chin instead of a point, features in the standard places, an optional double-chin
  line, a much wider neck. In 3/4 there is a larger gap from the mouth to the chin outline and a cheek line at the ear base.
- Child (p105-123): the lower half is split into 3 (brows on line 1, lashes on line 2). The eyes are about twice the teen
  size. The cheeks are squared off, running nearly vertically before angling to a blunt point. The mouth is midway from
  circle to chin. Ears can be a bit larger. Child profile: a tiny nose point, a short angled drop to the chin, and a nearly
  horizontal jaw underside. Pink blush ovals appear in colour (p105).

## eyes
- Eye construction (p13-17): three boxes slightly taller than wide, so one eye width of gap between the eyes. The lash
  lines tilt up toward the centre. The upper lash is a heavy curve that flicks out at the outer corner; the lower lash is a
  short thin stroke. The iris is a tall oval with a small round highlight at the top, an inner pupil oval, and a flattened
  bottom where it meets the lower lash. The two-line lid fold sits over the inner part of the eye (p29, p46).
- The highlight is on the same side in both eyes. Most eyes have at least one highlight, at the top, bottom or both (p126).
- The iris is darker at the top, under the lid (denser hatching at the top, p379). In the neutral eye the iris is tucked
  under the upper lid.
- 101 eyes (p125-128): semirealistic (small, narrow), cartoony (round, simple), big shojo eyes (many highlights, lashes),
  hyper-realistic, pupil-less (glassy), single-highlight, tiny-iris (menacing or shifty).
- 3/4: the far eye is narrower and sits against the far cheek (p36, p215). Profile: a narrow wedge between lines 2 and 3,
  just behind the face contour (p55).
- Male: thinner upper lash line with no lash ticks (p49). Female: thick lash line with small outer ticks (p32).

## brows
- Thin, gently arched strokes, a little longer than the eye, about one band above the lashes (p15, p28). Male brows are a
  touch thicker and flatter (p49). Shojo brows sit very high (p21).
- 3/4: the far brow shifts toward the centre and is not directly over the far eye (p36).
- Brows are the main carrier of expression (p132-141): tilt, distance to the lashes, crooks and zigzags.

## nose
- Teen: a tiny hook or tick resting on the circle bottom, on the centre line (p28). 3/4: a small angled hook projecting
  past the centre line (p36). Profile: a small sharp point on a straight slope (p54, p59).
- Adults get a full nose with bridge and nostrils (p70), and in 3/4 a long bridge with an angular underside (p80, p84).

## mouth
- Midway between the circle bottom (nose) and the chin tip (p28, p45, p110). Neutral: a short, slightly upturned line
  about one eye wide (p32, p49). In 3/4 it starts at the centre line and extends to the near side (p36). In profile it is a
  short stroke set back from the lip bumps (p55).
- Open mouths: a lens, oval or D with one flat interior tone (p131, p133, p138-140, p378). Adults add a lower-lip line (p81).

## teeth_tongue
- Teeth are one white upper band, sometimes with a small fang (p137, p231). Clenched teeth have a broken outline (p136).
  A tongue, when shown, is a simple lighter rounded shape (p237).

## ears
- Top at mid-eye, bottom just above the mouth line (p29, p46). Simple C shape with one or two inner curves. Realistic style
  puts them at eye level, very cartoony style at nose level (p19-20). In 3/4 only the near ear shows (p38). In profile the
  ear spans lines 2-4 against the vertical line (p55). In chibi the ear bottoms are level with the jaw (p215).

## jaw_chin_neck
- Teen neck: nearly straight lines starting inside the jaw, about half the face width (p32, p49). In 3/4 it runs from the
  ear/cheek junction to the chin tip (p38). In profile the throat starts halfway along the jaw underside (p56). Thicker for
  boys (p163), much wider for fuller types (p92). Adults get a neck line (p82).

## hair_framing
- Hair rises about 1/4 of the diameter above the skull, and its strands curve with the head (p30-31). Bangs part so they
  don't hide the eyes and reach about to the brows (p48). Hair stands well off the skull (chibi warning, p218). Short hair
  reads as male (p47). Hairstyle libraries on p60-64. Adults show more forehead and simpler styles (p72).

## face_shading
- The one shadow every lesson adds is under the chin, on the neck, following the jaw (p31, p39, p48, p58, p73, p83, p93, p103).
- In colour the skin is flat and the hair has a lighter highlight band (p20-21, p34). Blush is diagonal hatching or pink
  ovals on the cheeks (p105, p131, p140, p237). Keep the skin around the eyes and mouth free of lines on young faces (p74, p183).

## Expressions
- 12 common expressions (p129-141):
  - Cheerful: small gentle smile, eye bottoms flattened (p130).
  - Ecstatic joy: eyes shut as upward arcs, big D mouth, blush (p131).
  - Confusion: one brow lowered, one raised, tiny o mouth (p132).
  - Concern: brows slightly angled in, mild furrow (p133).
  - Sadness/regret: inner brow ends rise, heavy lids, tiny frown (p134).
  - Boredom: flat lids over at least half the iris, neutral brows (p135).
  - Determination: brows angled down and pressed to the lashes, clenched teeth (p136).
  - Anger: crooked brow ends, furrow marks, wide mouth with bared upper teeth, highlights gone (p137).
  - Distress: brows up with a zigzag, irises not touching the upper lids, tears, blush (p138).
  - Surprise: small floating irises, small gasping mouth (p139).
  - Embarrassment: upturned brows, big smile, wide eyes, heavy blush (p140).
  - Sadness/crying: big irises under the lids, one or two tears, wobbly lower lip (p141).
- Chibi rage lesson (p223-227): a zigzag brow, white circle eyes, a mouth reaching the chin, a cross vein, and vertical
  lines that never cross the eyes.
- 20 chibi emotions (p228-247): angry, shy (dot eyes), elated (^^), embarrassed (> < and grin), sassy (wink),
  tearful, in love (heart eyes), furious (vein), despondent (shut eyes with streaming tears), happy-energetic (^^ and blush
  ovals), bored (half lids), frustrated (squeezed shut and sweat drop), surprised (dot pupils), disappointed (closed eyes,
  sigh), devious (slit eyes and grin), disapproving (half lids and pout), shocked (blank white circles, huge mouth),
  upset (white eyes, tear, wavy frown), vulnerable (large pupil-less irises, blush), frightened (gloom lines, small irises).
- Sequencing (p392): play a calm neutral face against strongly changed reaction faces.
