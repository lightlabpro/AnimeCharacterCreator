# The Art of Metal Gear Solid - Yoji Shinkawa (SoftBank, 187 pp) - reading notes (q20)

Read: full text layer (every page; mostly captions, character dossiers and a long Shinkawa x Enki Bilal interview,
much of it Japanese/garbled OCR) + every page image (21 3x3 sheets, sketchbook face pages p141-151 viewed at
full size). An art book, not a tutorial: official/publicity/magazine/goods illustrations, character sheets,
mechanics (Metal Gear REX), environment artwork, sketchbook, costumes, storyboard, VFX directions, interview.

## Core content
- Style: brush-ink silhouettes, large flat blacks, very few lines; faces are a few strokes - brow/eye socket mass,
  nose ridge, mouth line - with shadow shapes carrying the form.
- Interview (p170-178): bring in realistic present-day items so the fiction feels real; "leave room" - Shinkawa
  draws characters with few lines so the viewer completes them; Bilal: too much detail makes the drawing static.
- Character sheets (p98-115): front / side / back turnarounds with head close-ups in front, 3/4 and profile; the
  profile face keeps the brow ridge forward of the eye and the eye as a short wedge set back.
- Sketchbook p151 (the 90s-anime female faces): in 3/4 the near eye is a large almond, the far eye is shorter but
  stays fully inside the face contour, separated from the cheek line by the nose bridge; the nose is a single
  short line with a tick of shadow; the mouth is a short line placed under the nose, not reaching the cheek.

## What this means for head_mhs3_n56 (q20)
- 3/4 far eye: keep it inside the face outline with a visible gap to the cheek edge (our known open issue: the far
  eye sits at the cheek edge). Near eye large but not stretched sideways.
- Lean on shadow shapes (the upper-lip band, mouth bottom) rather than more lines - consistent with the toon target.
