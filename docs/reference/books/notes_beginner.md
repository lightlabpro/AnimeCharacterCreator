# Drawing for the Absolute Beginner - Mark & Mary Willenbrink (131 pp) - reading notes (q20)

Read: full text layer (every page) + every page image (15 3x3 sheets).

## Content
- Materials (p8-15); structural, value, black-and-white (chiaroscuro) and contour sketches (p16-27).
- Principles (p28-49): basic shapes, gauging proportions with a locked arm, angles, references, linear perspective
  (horizon, vantage point, 1/2/3 point), atmospheric perspective, ellipses, arches/roofs, reflections.
- Values (p50-55): contrast is relative; value scale; light source, highlight, form shadow, cast shadow, reflected
  light; a light source at top left/right gives more depth than one straight above; light from below looks scary
  (the Frankenstein example, p54).
- Practice subjects (p56-79), figures (p80-83), faces (p84-87), composition (p88-92), demos (p93-123), glossary/index.

## Faces (p84-87) - generic adult proportions
- Front: head 5 eye-widths wide, 7 high; eyes halfway crown-chin; one eye-width between eyes; nose line a little less
  than half from eyes to chin; mouth line a little less than half from nose to chin; nose width = inner eye corners;
  ears from the brow line to the nose base.
- 3/4 (p85): egg with the chin off centre; eye line slightly off centre; the centre line through the middle of the eye
  line; same proportions; the nose bridge angles in toward the centre of the eyes then out to the nose base; ear top at
  the brows, bottom at the nose base.
- Side (p86): circle + vertical front line; eye halfway; the profile eye is a TRIANGLE about a third of the way in from
  the front edge of the circle; nose base a little under half eye-to-chin; mouth a little under half nose-to-chin; ear
  just behind the central vertical, from brow line to nose base; the jaw ends near the lower back of the ear.
- Individual faces (p87): women - same proportions, smaller ears/nose/jaw, larger lips; children - eyes BELOW the
  middle, big forehead, wide head, small features; elderly - larger ears and nose, longer chin.

## What this means for head_mhs3_n56 (q20)
- Profile eye = triangle set back from the profile (third rule in a 3rd book).
- Mouth line "a little less than halfway" nose-to-chin: the mouth sits nearer the nose than the chin. If our mouth
  is low, the lower face reads long (Sammy's "stretched down").
- Light: keep it from upper side, never straight above or below.
