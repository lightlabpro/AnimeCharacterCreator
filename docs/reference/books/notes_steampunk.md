## Core content
- Ch1: history (Shelley, Poe, Verne, Twain, Wells, Lovecraft; K. W. Jeter coined the term in 1987), Victorian
  inspiration, tools, pencil grades and hand positions, shape to form (add ellipses / planes), value scales,
  hatching/crosshatching/blending, textures (hair has flow and gathers into smaller forms under one light; scales,
  wood, metal reflections, cloth wrinkles compress as they turn away), linear vs atmospheric perspective,
  foreshortening with ellipses, two-point perspective, colour wheel, colored pencil and acrylic techniques,
  Photoshop basics (p5-21).
- Ch2 gadgetry step-by-steps: spider, Infolodeon computer, lightning gun (ellipse guides for cylinders), pipe organ
  (metal = reflected-light shapes, darkest at the cylinder centre), bison, speedster car in colored pencil (p23-51).
- Ch3 characters: proportions - male 7 to 7.5 heads, female 6.5 to 7; male an inverted triangle (shoulders wider),
  female a triangle (hips wider), female muscles less defined; varied body types define character; mannequin of
  cylinders for action; Victorian costume archetypes; digital colouring of soldier and steam lady (p53-61).
- Ch4-5: Nautilus, zeppelin, flying galleon, time machine scene, steam city in acrylic over a gesso-mounted print
  (p62-95).
