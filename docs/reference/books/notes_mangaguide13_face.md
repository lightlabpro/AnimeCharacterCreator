# The Complete Guide to Drawing Manga & Anime (Date Naoto, 13-week course, 197 pp) - head/face notes (mg13)

## Head shape and construction - p23, p28, p31-p44, p128, p139
- Front: circle with crosshair, inscribed square, side arcs trimmed -> near-vertical cheeks; jaw turns in at
  mouth level (p31).
- Vertical ratios (top of skull to chin): upper eyelid at 1/2; hairline halfway between skull top and upper
  lid; nose bottom halfway between upper lid and chin; mouth halfway between nose and chin (p31-32 key point).
  p23 variant: lower lid-to-chin split into thirds, nose then mouth.
- Hair adds ~1/8 head height of volume above the skull (p32).
- Cranium is wider than the face contour; joining face outline straight into the skull makes the face look too
  big (p34, p128). Profile: full back of head, no cliff (p36).
- Profile: same heights as front; ear on the vertical center line; forehead overhangs, slight dip at the bridge;
  neck slants forward; back of skull meets neck at about mouth height (p35).
- 3/4: square + circle guide; curved center line through the nose bridge and mouth; far cheek bulges between nose
  and mouth; ear in the middle of the side plane, lobe at nose level; head as a box with front and side planes;
  tilted crown is gently flattened (p37-41). Center-line curve echoes the contour (p41).
- Head is "P"-shaped in side view: big cranium, protruding forehead, sunk eyes (p139).
- Balance checks: level guides for eyes/brows, same gaze for both eyes, correct right-hand slant with ruler
  guides (p26, p28, p33, p42).

## Face by type / age / gender - p36, p88, p140
- Younger = lower lower-lid (taller eyes), mouth and chin raised, wider outline; crown/upper lid/nose fixed.
  Adult women 6.5 heads, HS girls 6, JHS 6 with higher mouth/jaw (p140).
- A 2D teen head has a real 5-year-old's proportions; a 2D grade-schooler a newborn's (p140).
- Males: smaller eyes, narrower face; shoulders ~2 heads vs ~1.5 for females (p88). Thin neck = shallow head
  front-to-back (p36).

## Eyes - p23, p31-32, p45-47
- Eye height ~1/3 of upper-lid-to-chin; one eye width between eyes (p23, p31).
- Iris is a bowl under a dome cornea; full circle in front, ellipse and shifted when turned; not a centered
  sticker (p45-46).
- Five lid blocks (upper lid, inner corner, outer upper flick, outer lower, lower lid); styles omit some (p46).
- Eye area is a hill: the far eye narrows, height stays the same; corners ~60 deg in front, changing with angle
  (p46-47). Lashes radiate and taper (p47). Profile eye = wedge (p47).
- Finish: double-lid crease line, lashes, iris highlights (p32).

## Brows - p31, p38, p41, p166
- About 1/4 of the hairline-to-lid distance above the eye; inner end past the inner eye corner (p31).
- 3/4: far brow ends at the center-line curve; brow and eye offset because the eye is recessed (p38, p41).
- Brows are movable; the "hero mask" model shows brow shapes for each emotion (p166).

## Nose - p38, p50
- Triangular cross-section, drawn as a minimal mark: tip, center line, nostril, side line or shadow (p50).

## Mouth / teeth / tongue - p36, p48-49
- Short line, equal corners, center always on the center line; no split line in 3/4; notch at tight angles (p48).
- Child = rounded, adult = straight / loose S (p48). Profile: upper lip forward of lower (p36).
- Open: upper lip first, lower curve under it; slightly open shows upper teeth, wide open shows both rows, the
  jaw drops, tongue low in the mouth (p48-49).

## Ears - p31, p38, p49
- Upper lid to nose bottom; glasses-temple trick for placement; six-corner rim simplification (p31, p49).

## Jaw, chin, neck - p32, p39, p43-44, p162
- Neck sides under the inner iris edges; neck ~1/4 head long; shoulders ~1.5 ear-to-ear (p32).
- Sphere head + face shield + cylinder neck; underside of chin visible, often in solid shadow (p43).
- 3/4 neck lines parallel, neck starts at the ear base; a straight neck erases the jaw (p39, p44).
- Head tilt/turn raises that side's shoulder (p162).

## Hair framing - p105-p114
- Three blocks (bangs, middle, back) (p106); veil of ribbons with the face cut out (p109); flow from the part (p111).
- Locks narrow toward the sides, overlap front-over-back, highlight band follows a latitude; banana cross-section
  (p110). Varied bundle sizes and random tips; weight at the tip = soft (p105, p108).
- Weight position low = calm, high = lively; out-/in-silhouette; bangs and knot placement change style (p111-114).

## Face shading - p32, p131, p163
- Main shadow is the neck cast shadow under the jaw; face mostly unshaded; pink cheek blush (p32, p131).
- Head tilted down gets shadow over the upper face (p163).

## Expressions - p163-p166
- Three linked parts: brows, lids, mouth. Lower brows push the upper lid down (stronger eyes, lower lid rises);
  raised brows lift the lid (eyes open); mouth corners up/down raise/drop the lower lid (p165).
- Height alone changes emotion: brows close to eyes = serious/angry; brows raised away = fun/laugh; lower mouth =
  relaxed/goofy (p165).
- Mask brow shapes: angry V, worried (inner ends up), calm flat, surprised high arch (p166).
- Corners down = displeased, straight = blank, up = smile; always draw corners (p166).
- Wink/giggle, demure hand-to-mouth laugh, surprised wide-open mouth (p164). Head up = outward emotion, down =
  inward (p163).
- Not covered: embarrassment/blush as emotion, fear, crying, smugness, tears or sweat marks.
