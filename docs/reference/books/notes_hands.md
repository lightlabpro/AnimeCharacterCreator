# Drawing Dynamic Hands - Burne Hogarth (142 pp) - reading notes (q20)

Read: full text layer (every page) + every page image (16 3x3 sheets). This is a hand book; the head work only
uses its general form principles and its hand-to-face measures. The notes are kept because hands are a future asset.

## Content
- Ch1 Forms (p7-20): the hand is not a flat 2D shape but a 3D body form; contraposed masses give crest-and-trough
  rhythm; the palm wedge is the governing form; palm scoop (hollow palm, rounded back); thenar/hypothenar/knuckle
  pads; thumb triangle; latitudinal arcs; rod-and-ball fingers.
- Ch2 Proportions (p21-36): palm (wrist centre to middle-finger base) = middle finger length; each phalanx half the
  one before; index = ring; little-finger tip at the last ring knuckle; thumb at 25-30 deg; ulna head lines up with
  little knuckle; arcs through knuckles in depth.
  - HAND TO FACE (p35-36): palm base at the chin -> fingers reach the brow, middle finger touches the hairline; a hand
    laid sideways on the brow fits between hairline and eyebrows; palm sideways from nose base reaches the chin base.
- Ch3 Anatomy (p37-48): carpals, metacarpals, phalanges; joints and movement range; muscles; veins lie in hollows.
- Ch4 Landmarks (p49-60): olecranon, condyles, radius head, ulna head, styloid, snuffbox, lunate, tendons, thenar
  egg, hypothenar plateau, mid-palm trench, wedge fingertip pads, skin stress flows.
- Ch5 Movement (p61-78): palm rotation 180 deg; bends 90/45 deg; fist closure order; thumb angles.
- Ch6 Foreshortening (p79-90): cylinders become circles as they turn toward you; fingernail ellipses as depth cues;
  TONAL CHANGES TO ACHIEVE DEPTH (p82): advancing (top) surfaces light, receding side planes intermediate,
  regressing underplanes darkest, with accented dark contours on undersurfaces; overlapping, interlacing, spiraling;
  cast shadows indicate overlapping forms; expansion and compression.
- Ch7-11 (p91-138): inventing hands with parallel projection, the hand as instrument, gesture, aging (infant to 70+),
  occupations.

## What this means for head_mhs3_n56 (q20)
- The three-value plane rule (top light / side mid / underplane dark) is the same as Mattesi's lower jaw plane and
  Edwards' light logic: the teeth band, tongue and upper lip need a top/side/under value split to stop reading flat.
- Hand-face measures (hairline-brow = one hand width; nose base to chin = palm width) give an extra check for the
  lower face length when hands are added to the library.
