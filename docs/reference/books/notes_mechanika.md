# Mechanika (Revised & Updated) - Doug Chiang (IMPACT 2015, 248 epub pages) - reading notes (q20)

Read: full text layer (every page, 2 chunks) + every page image (7 combined sheets). A sci-fi vehicle / robot /
creature design book; no human face construction. Useful as design-evaluation method, not anatomy.

## Core content
- Tools, markers (work light to dark, fast, round to flat, large to small; markers dry lighter), blue-line and
  Post-it thumbnails that force big shapes over detail (p12-23).
- Foundation lines: perspective, construction, action lines; kept in the final drawing for energy (p24-26).
- The keys to good design (p29-31): 1 strong silhouette (squint; details embellish but don't determine),
  2 three-second rule (readable in three seconds), 3 personality, 4 functionality, 5 believability, 6 cool
  factor.
- Spaceships, fighter rendering: keep the foreground part light, darken the rest to pull the eye forward (p36);
  black margin thickest along the bottom grounds a drawing (p37); reflective surfaces carry a dark hard horizon
  band, warm on the sun side, cool opposite (p56-60, p198).
- Creatures: "Small alterations to familiar objects are all you need... Restraint is often the secret" (p64);
  an alien stays readable by giving it a recognisable head = personality (p71); organic line = light pen
  pressure and slight variation; line quality signals hard vs soft material (p66-67); detail and tone start from
  the head, the main focus (p76, p171).
- Darker tones create shadows that push lighter shapes forward; add dark tone where an area reads confusingly
  (p77, p130).
- Leave parts unfinished - define two or three of four identical legs and suggest the rest (p109, p153, p164).
- Positive and negative shapes: negative shapes create the silhouette (p103, p185, p211).
- Checks: squint (p69, p177), flip horizontally or mirror to catch errors (p89), postage-stamp thumbnail
  (p221), black-and-white value layer (p179). Vary edge quality - sharpest at the focal point (p91, p179, p220).
- Digital painting demos (Photoshop: hard/soft round, color dodge, soft light texture, masks) and portfolio tips
  (p27-28, p181-243).

## What this means for head_mhs3_n56 (q20)
- Our head boards should pass the silhouette and three-second tests at thumbnail size: the profile and 3/4
  silhouettes must read "young anime face" before any detail (p29, p221). Current complaint - "lower part of
  the face feels stretched down" - is a silhouette problem, so check it in pure black silhouette.
- Restraint (p64): small deliberate changes over large ones when adjusting the mouth/jaw.
- Flip / mirror check for symmetry errors (p89) - same as Lohan p18.
- Dark shadow pushes lighter shapes forward (p77): an upper-lip shadow and the mouth-interior dark make the lips
  and teeth read as in front, i.e. give depth.
