# Anime face rules from online tutorials (q19, 2026-10-16)

Collected for the MHS3-style head library; each rule is paraphrased, with its source. Numbers are the tutorials'
own (HW = head width, EH = eye height).

## Proportions (front)
- Eye line at or just below the head's vertical midpoint (0.50-0.55 of head height from the top); the top of the eyes
  touches the half line (AnimeOutline male head; Biorust).
- Nose base halfway between the top of the eyes (or the brow line) and the chin (AnimeOutline; Biorust).
- Bottom lip halfway between the nose base and the chin; the mouth line just above it. With the head split into
  eighths, the bottom lip (red part) sits about one eighth above the chin (AnimeOutline mouths, side view).
- Eye width 0.22-0.25 HW, one eye width between the eyes (Biorust).
- Adult jaw 1.1-1.2 cranium radii below the skull ball; male jaw ~10% longer, squarer chin, eyes ~20% shorter, heavier
  brow (Biorust). Male chin flat at the very end, not a sharp point (AnimeOutline 3/4 male).
- Ears between the brow line and the nose base; ear height about 1/4 of the head; nose bottom level with the ear bottom
  (AnimeOutline 3/4 male; Dessindigo).

## Three-quarter view
- Far eye 60-70% of the near eye's width; the gap between the eyes 0.6-0.7 eye width measured on the curve; the near
  eye sits much farther from the centre line than the far eye (Biorust; AnimeOutline 3/4 male).
- The far cheek line pushes out past the far eye; the near cheek is a contour from below the near eye to the chin. At
  about 60 degrees the nose crosses the far cheek line and the far eye tucks behind the bridge (Biorust).
- Mouth: the curve is asymmetric - its centre shifts toward the turn, the near side is longer and the far side
  shorter; most of the mouth lies on the near side of the centre line (AnimeOutline mouths; 3/4 male).
- Near brow wider and farther from the centre line than the far brow (AnimeOutline 3/4 male).

## Profile
- The eye becomes a wedge 0.12-0.15 HW wide (about half its front width) with the iris at the front edge; the far eye
  is hidden (Biorust); the iris turns into an oval (Clip Studio side profile; AnimeOutline side view).
- Nose tip a little above 1/4 of the head height from the chin; the bottom lip 1/8 above the chin; the underside of
  the nose another 1/8 above the bottom lip; lips touch a straight line from the nose tip to the chin (AnimeOutline).
- The side-view mouth is about half the front mouth, set back behind the nose; the top lip can be a bit bigger
  (AnimeOutline; Clip Studio).
- Jaw: one line from the ear down and forward to the chin; ear centred on the skull's vertical midline (Biorust).

## Mouths, teeth, tongue
- Teeth are one combined shape or with hints of individual teeth; the top row stays fixed to the head, the bottom row
  moves with the jaw; wide-open mouths show fewer teeth than slightly open ones (lips cover them) (AnimeOutline teeth,
  side mouths).
- The jaw hinges down and slightly forward, it does not drop straight down (AnimeOutline side mouths).

## Shading (cel)
- Flat tones, hard edges; general light: shadows under the nose (one side), eyelid creases, under the head on the neck;
  top light: upper lip lit, under-nose and under-lip in shadow; upper irises darker (AnimeOutline shading; 3/4 male).

Sources: animeoutline.com (male head and face; side-view face; mouths; side-view mouths; teeth; 3/4 male face;
shading in different lighting), biorust.com/tutorials/face-proportions-anime, dessindigo.com (three-quarter face),
tips.clip-studio.com/en-us/articles/8004 (side profile).

## Ears (q20 follow-up, AnimeOutline "How to Draw Anime & Manga Ears" and "Female Face 3/4 View")
- Anime ears sit lower than real ears: standard method places them between the head's horizontal midline and a
  line halfway from that midline to the chin; chibi/stylised places them lower still.
- Profile: ear behind the head's vertical midline with a small gap; side view is where the rim, opening and inner
  split read best.
- Detail level follows style: semi-realistic = rim + one bump + opening; simplified = outline + rim + bump.
  Our MHS3-style ear should be the semi-realistic set, not full anatomy.
- 3/4 female face: ear between the top of the eyes and the bottom of the nose, about a quarter of the face tall.
- 3/4: the far eye, far brow and far side of the lips are drawn vertically shorter than the near ones (in addition
  to narrower), and compress more the further the head turns.
- Vertical spacing: nose halfway between the eye tops and the chin; bottom lip halfway between nose base and
  chin, top lip just above. A lower face that reads "stretched down" breaks this halving.
