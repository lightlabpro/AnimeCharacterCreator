# Style dataset

This folder is an extra reference for the asset-library prompt in [../CLAUDE_BUILD_PROMPT.md](../CLAUDE_BUILD_PROMPT.md). Read `entries.json`, then open the matching image. Keep using online references and your own knowledge as well. None of the three replaces the others.

Each entry names what the frame is for, such as hair clumps, layered armor, scale plates, pointed ears, or a humanoid dragon body. The limit on every entry is the same: use the trait, and do not reproduce the character, costume, monster, or emblem.

The base look is anime in the manner of Monster Hunter Stories 3. Breath of Fire 3 and 4, and Mega Man Legends 2 and 3, are variations selected by the Stories, Breath, and Legends shader presets. Frames `ref-08`, `ref-09`, and `ref-10` are shape references for beast people and dragon people. They are not Monster Hunter Stories frames.
